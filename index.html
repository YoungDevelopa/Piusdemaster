An engineering partner, not a parts shop

PIUS De Master is an engineering workshop in Nanyuki Town, Kenya. We turn customer ideas, technical drawings, samples, damaged components and practical problems into engineered solutions, from small custom jobs to larger industrial enquiries.

We sell engineering services and manufacturing capability. We do not sell workshop machines or equipment.
